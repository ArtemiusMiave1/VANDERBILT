VANDERBILT README

Controls:
WASD–Move
TAB-Pull up Clipboard
Left Click–Pick Up/Drop
R–Restart
Esc- Settings Menu

Instructions all in game. 
Read Tutorial Book on Desk, marked with yellow arrow.
For hints about Map Icons, Request Conditions, check the Open Book on the Desk (Vanderbilt Handbook). 



In this build of Vanderbilt, you play through a mockup of a first day as the captain of the Vanderbilt Courier Delivery Service. Your shift goes from 8am-4pm (8min). Fulfill Requests accordingly and make sure you deliver them on time. Collect your money at Headquarters, watch your fuel, route carefully around/push through storms(marked purple) and complete as many requests as you can! 

At the end of your shift, your score will be calculated. 

To Buy Resources- Go to the Crate Icon on the map and buy at the Fax Machine
To Collect Money- Go to the Eagle Icon(Vanderbilt Headquarters) 



If you encounter any bugs, press R to restart.
